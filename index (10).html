English

Background and Early Life

Ilfinesh was born in 1962 in Guuto Giddaa, East Wallaga, Oromia, Ethiopia. She showed a strong interest and talent in traditional singing and poetry from an early age. She began her artistic journey with Kinet Guddattu Wallaggaa and later gained recognition on stages in Nekemte and Addis Ababa.

Afaan Oromoo

Hojii Muuziqaa fi Gahee Aadaa

Ilfinesh Qannoo muuziqaa Oromoo, geerarsa fi walaloo qabsoo walitti fiduun seenaa fi eenyummaa uummataa ibsaa turte. Sirboonni ishee Afaan Oromoo kabajuu, aadaa tiksuu, bilisummaa fi mirga namoomaa irratti xiyyeeffatan.

Muuxannoo Aartii

Bara 1980moota: Sirna Dargii keessatti sirboota aadaa fi jaalala biyyaa dhiyeessite. Sirbi “Chim-Chim Goona” Abitew Kebede waliin sirbite beekamtii argate.

Bara 1990moota: Albamii “Saahilee Dagaagoo” bara 1994 waraabdee uummata biraan geesse.

English

Music and Cultural Contribution

Ilfinesh brought Oromo music, traditional geerarsa and resistance poetry together. Her work celebrated the Oromo language and cultural heritage, while addressing freedom, human rights and collective identity.

1980s: She performed traditional and patriotic songs during the Derg era. Her collaboration with Abitew Kebede on “Chim-Chim Goona” became well known.

1990s: She recorded the cassette album “Saahilee Dagaagoo” in 1994, sharing her music with a wider audience.

Qabsoo Mirga Namoomaa fi Bilisummaa

Hojii aartii ishee meeshaa sagalee eenyummaa, mirga namoomaa fi bilisummaa taasisuun beekamti. Odeeffannoon seenaa ishee yeroo adda addaatti hidhaa, dararaa fi dhiibbaa ishee irra ga'e ni ibsa. Haata'u malee, hojii aartii fi dhaamsa ishee itti fufsiisuun kutannoo agarsiifte.

English

Advocacy, Resistance and Human Rights

Her art was more than entertainment: it became a voice for identity, human rights and freedom. Accounts of her life describe imprisonment, mistreatment and pressure during different political periods. Despite these hardships, she continued to stand by her artistic voice and message.

Dhaamsa / A Message of Unity

Dhaamsa Tokkummaa Oromoo

“Reeffi koo uummata Oromoo tokkoomsuu yoo danda'e Ambootti na awwaalaa. Yoo danda'uu baate garuu, qorri biyya Norway na haa nyaatu.”

“If my burial in Ambo can unite the Oromo people, bury me in Ambo. If it cannot, let the cold of Norway take me.”

Her Legacy
